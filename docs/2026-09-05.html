For VC allocators, the throughline is that capital is still pouring into AI infrastructure and applied-AI bets at record pace (per Crunchbase's August funding surge and this week's Crusoe/Fluidstack rounds), even as the legal, safety, and regulatory bill for the previous generation of AI deployment — copyright, agent safety, autonomous vehicle oversight — comes due in parallel.
