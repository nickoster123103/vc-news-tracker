For venture investors, the throughline is a bifurcation: infrastructure and tooling investment around AI remains extremely hot (Parallel, Positron, Verda, Firecrawl), while diligence around frontier-model governance, safety incidents, and litigation exposure is becoming more important as courts, contracts, and settlements — rather than clear regulatory frameworks — increasingly dictate how AI and platform companies must operate.
