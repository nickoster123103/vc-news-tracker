The IPO market is the other throughline. Crunchbase's framing — that the 2026 IPO window is "opening selectively" for companies that used the slowdown to shore up financial reporting and governance — is borne out by today's news that OpenAI has delayed its own IPO over AI safety concerns and is instead seeking another $30B in private capital. Meanwhile, a wave of SPAC and S-1 filings over the trailing month (BHAV Acquisition Corp II, MNBS, Orange Street, Calm Seas, TCGX II, Frazier Life Sciences II, among others) hints that shell vehicles are stockpiling capital in anticipation of that selective reopening.

The net effect: capital and ambition are sprinting ahead of governance, and today's headlines show both forces — money and oversight — accelerating at once.
